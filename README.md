# Advanced Robotics and Autonomous Systems for Next-Gen Renewable and Nuclear Energy Infrastructure

## Applications of Robotics in Energy Generation, Inspection, Maintenance, and Safety

This repository contains my ongoing research on the application of **advanced robotics and autonomous systems to next-generation renewable and nuclear energy infrastructure**.

The research investigates how robotic systems can be used across the energy lifecycle, from energy generation and infrastructure monitoring to inspection, maintenance, repair, and safety operations.

The work focuses on the growing role of robotics in energy infrastructure where conventional human-operated methods can become difficult, expensive, dangerous, or inefficient.

The research combines concepts from:

- Mechanical Engineering
- Robotics
- Autonomous Systems
- Renewable Energy
- Nuclear Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence
- Computer Vision
- Control Systems
- CAD and Mechanical Design
- Simulation
- Software Engineering
- Energy Systems
- Industrial Automation

> **Status: Ongoing Research**

This is an active research project. The research paper is continuously being updated as new literature, technologies, engineering concepts, datasets, mathematical models, CAD designs, simulations, and research findings are investigated.

# Research Overview

The global energy sector is undergoing significant changes.

Renewable energy technologies such as:

- Solar power
- Wind energy
- Hydropower
- Offshore wind
- Geothermal energy

are expanding alongside renewed interest in nuclear energy as part of the transition toward reliable and lower-carbon energy systems.

At the same time, energy infrastructure is becoming increasingly complex.

Modern energy facilities contain large numbers of mechanical, electrical, structural, and control systems that require continuous monitoring and maintenance.

Many of these systems operate in environments that can be:

- High temperature
- High pressure
- Radioactive
- Offshore
- Underwater
- At height
- Difficult to access
- Chemically hazardous
- Structurally dangerous
- Remote

These conditions create opportunities for robotic systems.

Instead of relying entirely on human personnel, robots can increasingly perform inspection, monitoring, data collection, maintenance assistance, and potentially autonomous intervention.

This research investigates how advanced robotic and autonomous systems can support the future of energy infrastructure.

# Research Objective

The main objective of this research is to investigate the application of robotics and autonomous systems to the **generation, inspection, maintenance, monitoring, and safety of renewable and nuclear energy infrastructure**.

The research aims to:

1. Investigate current applications of robotics in energy infrastructure.
2. Examine robotic systems used in renewable energy facilities.
3. Investigate robotic systems used in nuclear energy facilities.
4. Identify energy infrastructure where robotic intervention can provide significant benefits.
5. Analyse the use of autonomous robots for inspection and monitoring.
6. Investigate robotic maintenance systems.
7. Examine AI and computer vision for defect detection.
8. Investigate autonomous navigation in complex industrial environments.
9. Explore robotic manipulation and intervention.
10. Investigate the use of drones and aerial robots for energy infrastructure.
11. Investigate underwater robotics for offshore energy systems.
12. Examine mobile and legged robots for industrial inspection.
13. Investigate robotic systems for nuclear environments.
14. Examine the role of digital twins and simulation.
15. Investigate ROS 2 and robotics simulation platforms.
16. Analyse the potential contribution of AI to autonomous energy infrastructure management.
17. Identify future research opportunities for intelligent energy infrastructure.
